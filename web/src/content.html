Inside the Transformer

No background needed. We follow one sentence, "the cat sat on the mat because it was tired", and work out, number by number, how a Transformer figures out that "it" means the cat. Every idea is a one-minute step you can poke, predict and replay.

How it works

Each lesson is 7–11 tiny steps. Each step is one of: Learn an idea in plain words, Try it with one interaction, Predict before the answer is revealed, Watch an animation you can pause and scrub, or The math with every number worked out. Every step ends with "What just happened?". Your place is remembered in this browser.

The Lab (left) has a real trained Transformer, a date translator, to explore once the ideas click. Your notebooks in ~/Desktop/Transformer/notebooks/ rebuild everything in PyTorch.
